# ZOrbit

ZOrbit is a radial brush menu for ZBrush. Press **Q** over ZBrush and a ring of your favourite brushes and buttons appears around the cursor. Flick toward one and let go, and ZBrush switches to it. You choose what goes on the ring in a small editor called ZOrbit Studio. It is free, open source (GPL-3.0), and runs on Windows.

If you know pie menus from Blender or marking menus from Maya, ZOrbit brings that to ZBrush.

![Holding Q: the ring opens around the cursor, the cursor hovers two brushes, the More Brushes submenu opens on hover, and a flick picks ClayTubes](images/open-animation.gif)

## Under heavy development

ZOrbit is under heavy development. New versions come out often, so screens and settings can move between them. Feedback is welcome, including "I didn't get what this button does". Open an issue at [github.com/combwizard/zorbit/issues](https://github.com/combwizard/zorbit/issues).
